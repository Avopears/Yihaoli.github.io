## About Me

Dr. Yihao Li is an Assistant Professor in the School of Integrated Circuits at Nanjing University of Science and Technology. His research focuses on terahertz technologies, metastructures, and intelligent photonic systems, with particular interests in terahertz devices, diffractive optical computing, and computational approaches for sensing, imaging, and information processing. He received his B.Eng. degree from the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC) in 2020 and his Ph.D. degree in Engineering from Tsinghua University in 2025 under Professor Xiaoping Zheng. During his doctoral study, he established close research collaborations with the Suzhou Institute of Nano-Tech and Nano-Bionics, Chinese Academy of Sciences, focusing on advanced terahertz detectors and semiconductor device technologies. From February to July 2025, he was a joint Ph.D. student in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at the National University of Singapore under Professor Chengkuo Lee, where he conducted research on metasurfaces and electromagnetic-wave manipulation. His current research spans terahertz diffractive deep neural networks (D2NNs), reconfigurable metastructures, intelligent terahertz sensing and imaging, and spatial photonic computing. By integrating electromagnetic structure design, functional devices, and computational algorithms, his work aims to develop intelligent photonic systems that bridge physical hardware and artificial intelligence, enabling next-generation solutions for terahertz sensing, imaging, and information processing.

## Research Interests

1. **Terahertz Detectors:** Development of GaN HEMT-based direct detectors integrated with metastructures to explore the performance limits of terahertz detection.
2. **Diffractive Neural Networks:** Design of functional diffractive deep neural networks (D2NNs) operating at terahertz and visible wavelengths.
3. **AI-Driven Design of Devices and Functional Structures:** Design and optimization of devices and functional structures using deep neural networks.
